## Full description

Sort Rush is a satisfying color-sorting puzzle built for one thumb and a spare minute. Tap a tube to pick up its top color, tap another to pour it in — line up matching colors until every tube holds just one solid color. Levels get trickier the further you go, with fresh procedurally generated boards every time, so there's always another puzzle waiting. Stuck? Undo a move, grab a hint, or add an extra tube to give yourself room to think. Climb the leaderboard, keep your daily streak alive, and see how far you can sort.

## How to play

1. **Tap a tube** to pick up the color sitting on top.
2. **Tap another tube** to pour it in — it only works if that tube is empty or already has the same color on top.
3. **Clear a tube** by filling it completely with a single color.
4. **Win the level** once every tube is either empty or one solid color.
5. Out of moves? Use **Undo** to take back your last move, or watch a quick ad for a **Hint** (highlights a valid move) or an **Extra Tube** (adds more room to work with).
6. Clear levels to earn 💎 gems, keep your daily streak going, and climb the 🏆 leaderboard.

## Key features

- Endless, procedurally generated levels with increasing difficulty
- Pure tap controls — no timing, no swiping, one-thumb friendly
- Undo, Hint, and Extra Tube helpers when you get stuck
- Gems, stars, and a daily play streak for long-term progression
- Leaderboard ranked by highest level reached
